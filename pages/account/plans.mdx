# Plans

PinArkive offers cumulative tiers: each plan includes everything in the previous tier plus the extras listed below. **Taxes not included** on all paid prices. Annual billing is **20% off** (pay monthly × 12 × 0.8).

Sign up and manage billing in the dashboard: [app.pinarkive.com](https://app.pinarkive.com/register).

---

## Pricing

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (20% off) |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Free** | Free | — |
| **Starter** | €2.99 | €28.70 (€2.39/mo) |
| **Developer** | €9.99 | €95.90 (€7.99/mo) |
| **Professional** | €29.99 | €287.90 (€23.99/mo) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom |

## Limits by plan

Storage uses binary units: **1 GB = 1024³ bytes** (displayed below as rounded GB/MB).

| Limit | Free | Starter | Developer | Professional | Enterprise |
|-------|------|---------|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Storage | 50 MB | 1 GB | 20 GB | 100 GB | Custom |
| Max file size | 25 MB | 500 MB | 2 GB | 10 GB | Custom |
| Uploads per second (per API key)* | 1 | 2 | 10 | 25 | Custom |
| Gateway bandwidth / month* | 1 GB | 10 GB | 100 GB | 500 GB | Custom |
| Download retention per file* | Account lifetime | 7 days | 30 days | 90 days (+ CSV/API) | Custom |
| Timelocks | — | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Support | Email (all plans) | Email | Email | Priority + advanced metrics | Priority + advanced metrics |

\* Enforced by the API according to your plan (rate limits, gateway metering, per-CID download counters). Exact enforcement is plan-dependent on the backend.

**Timelocks** are available from **Developer** (€9.99/mo) and above. Community chat (e.g. Telegram) is not official support.

**Professional** and **Enterprise** include priority support and **advanced metrics** (dashboards and operational insight — not basic status charts only).

---

## Upgrades and billing

- View or change your plan in the app under **Plans** after logging in at [app.pinarkive.com](https://app.pinarkive.com).
- The API returns **403** when a plan limit is exceeded (storage, file size, feature flags such as timelocks or regional clusters).
- There is no public unauthenticated “list all plans” JSON for every field above; dashboard and checkout reflect live pricing.
